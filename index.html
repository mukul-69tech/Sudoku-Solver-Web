Welcome to Apex Sudoku

How to Play:

  • Select an empty cell in the grid, then click a number on your keyboard (1-9) or use the on-screen **Input Controller**.
  • Press `Backspace`, `Delete`, or the `Eraser` button on the controller to clear a cell.
  • Navigate the board easily using your keyboard's **arrow keys**.

Key Features:

  • AI Visualizer: Switch to the AI Visualizer tab and click **Visual Solve** to watch the backtracking algorithm execute in real-time. Adjust the speed slider to see individual recursive decisions.
  • Input Validation: Cells containing duplicate numbers in the same row, column, or 3x3 grid will automatically highlight in red.
  • Hints: Need help? Click **Hint** to place the correct number in your currently selected cell.
  • Synthesized Audio: Sound effects are synthesized live using the browser's Web Audio API—no network downloads needed.
